# Service pages

## seo-specialist
**Positioning:** Core service offer — broad enough to rank for the head term, specific enough to
tell clients exactly how you work.
**Internal-linking role:** Parent service page that funnels internal links to more specific offers
like local SEO, audits, and technical SEO.

## local-seo (Maps & GBP Specialist)
**Positioning:** Speak directly to service businesses that need cleaner service-area targeting,
GBP support, and pages that convert local intent.
**Internal-linking role:** Connect the local SEO offer with niche case studies such as plumbing,
detailing, gutters, and law.

## technical-seo
**Positioning:** The work hidden beneath rankings — crawl paths, canonical signals, rendering
issues, internal architecture, and template QA.
**Tone:** Implementation-focused. Clients want clarity on risk, sequencing, and what gets fixed.

## seo-audits
**Positioning:** Sell the value of a sharper audit — not more screenshots, but clearer decisions
about what to fix, publish, or consolidate next.
**Sales note:** In proposals, frame the audit as a decision document, not a giant spreadsheet.

## wordpress-design
**Positioning:** For clients who need a WordPress designer that understands search intent,
information architecture, and lead generation — not design alone.
**Tone:** Strongest when it positions design as a business system, not just a visual refresh.

## wordpress-seo-expert
**Positioning:** Bridge strategy and implementation for clients who need search-focused WordPress
updates that improve rankings, UX, and lead generation.
**Use when:** Clients need one person who connects SEO direction with real WordPress implementation.
